# UC Guard

UC Guard is an Android client built on the open-source Aether core by CluvexStudio
(https://github.com/CluvexStudio/Aether), used under the GNU AGPL-3.0.
UC Guard is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Aether project, and does not use
its name or branding (see TRADEMARK.md). The full source of UC Guard is published in
this repository under the same license.

It also bundles hev-socks5-tunnel (MIT, https://github.com/heiher/hev-socks5-tunnel),
Psiphon tunnel-core (GPL-3.0) and lyrebird (BSD).
